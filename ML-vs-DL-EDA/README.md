## Exploratory Data Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is an important step in data analysis where we explore, summarize, and visualize data to understand its structure, detect patterns, identify anomalies, test assumptions, and check relationships between variables before applying any machine learning or statistical models.

### Types of EDA

1. **Univariate Analysis:** It studies one variable at a time to undestand it's characteristics and distribution.

2. **Bivariate Analysis:** It examines the relationship between two variables to understand how they interact or influence each other.

3. **Multivariate Analysis:** It studies two or more variables together to find the underlying complex relationship between them within the dataset.

### Steps to perform EDA

1. **Understanding the problem and the data:** Understand the problem we want to solve and what information the dataset contains.

2. **Collecting and inspecting the data:** Gather the required data and check its rows, columns, data types, and structure.

3. **Handling missing data:** Identify missing values in the dataset and fill or remove them as needed.

4. **Exploring data characteristics:** Study the data using statistical measures like mean, median, mode, and standard deviation to understand its properties.

5. **Performing data transformation:** Convert data into a suitable format by changing data types, encoding categorical values, or scaling numerical values.

6. **Visualizing relationship of data:** Use graphs and charts to understand patterns and relationships between variables.

7. **Handling outliers:** Identify unusual values that are very different from the other values and decide how to handle them.

8. **Communicate findings and insights:** Summarize the important observations and patterns discovered during the analysis.
